# Figure legends: PG reviewer figures

Positions are numbered from the first residue after the initiator methionine, so the P/G–D/E/T motif sits at positions 1–2 and the figures start at position 3.

---

**Figure 15b | Residues enriched downstream of the P/G–D/E/T motif in UBR3 substrates.**
Significance logo for positions 3–23. The 20 UBR3 substrates carrying an N-terminal P/G–D/E/T motif were compared with the 193 peptides in the screen that carry the same motif but were not stabilised on UBR3 loss. Letter height and colour both show −log10 *P* (two-sided Fisher's exact test, one test per residue and position). Only residues enriched in substrates at *P* < 0.05 are drawn. *P* values are not corrected for multiple testing: 18 of 416 testable residue–position combinations reach *P* < 0.05, close to the ~21 expected by chance, and none remain significant after Benjamini–Hochberg correction. The strongest signals are arginine at positions 6, 7 and 16.
A second version shows positions 3–11 only. It uses the same *P* values, and the correction still covers all positions from 3 to 23.

**Figure 16c | Position-by-residue enrichment in UBR3 substrates.**
Heatmap of the 20 amino acids (rows, grouped by chemical class) at positions 3–23 (columns). The groups and test are as in Fig. 15b. Colour shows −log10 *P* for residues enriched in substrates; depleted residues are shown in the lowest colour. Numbers show the fold change (frequency in substrates / frequency in non-substrates) for every cell with *P* < 0.05, with stars above: \**P* < 0.05, \*\**P* < 0.01, \*\*\**P* < 0.001 (uncorrected). Grey cells, residue absent from both groups at that position (no test possible). No depleted residue reached *P* < 0.05 at any position. None of the cells remain significant after Benjamini–Hochberg correction.
A second version shows positions 3–11 only, with *P* values and correction as in the full figure.

**Figure 17b | Enrichment of chemical classes at each position in UBR3 substrates.**
Heatmap of six residue classes (rows) at positions 3–23 (columns): basic (K, H, R), acidic (D, E), aromatic (F, Y, W), aliphatic (G, P, A, M), hydrophobic (V, L, I) and polar (S, T, C, N, Q). The groups and test are as in Fig. 15b. Colour shows −log10 *P*. Numbers show the fold change for every cell with *P* < 0.05, with stars as in Fig. 16c. 4 of 126 class–position combinations reach *P* < 0.05; basic residues at position 7 are the only one that remains significant after Benjamini–Hochberg correction (9/20 substrates vs 21/193 non-substrates, 4.1-fold, *q* = 0.049).
A second version shows positions 3–11 only, with *P* values and correction as in the full figure.
